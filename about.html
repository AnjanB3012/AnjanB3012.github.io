About Me

My name is Anjan Bellamkonda, and I am a student with a deep interest in technology and its potential to solve real-world problems. Born and raised in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, I moved to the United States a year ago under my father's H1B visa and graduated high school in May 2024. I am now preparing to attend Virginia Tech, where I plan to pursue general engineering with a focus on computer science. Growing up, my father, a software engineer, introduced me to coding challenges and aptitude questions, which nurtured my passion for problem-solving and analytical thinking. My journey has been shaped by diverse experiences, from winning a hackathon for an AI-based project to adapting to a new culture and education system in the United States. I've worked on projects like an AI email summarizer using Gemini AI and a sentiment analysis study during significant political events. These experiences have not only honed my technical skills but also deepened my understanding of the societal impact of AI. Looking forward, I am excited to further explore artificial intelligence and work on innovative solutions that make a meaningful impact.
